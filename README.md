# Culinary Dreams Academy

Build a modern, professional, and appetizing website for a professional culinary and fast food training center called "Académie Master Class (AMC)" located in Kélibia, Tunisia (68 Rue ali belhouane, Kélibia, 8090).

### Target Audience:
- Aspiring chefs and professionals looking for state-approved diplomas in culinary arts and pastry.
- Individuals looking for specialized, hands-on fast-food and pizzaïolo training to boost their career or open their own business.

### Design & Theme:
- Color Palette: Professional culinary colors (rich dark tones combined with warm gold/amber accents and crisp white) to reflect high-end culinary arts.
- Style: Sleek, mouth-watering, clean layout, highly responsive for mobile and desktop users.

### Key Sections & Pages Needed:
1. Homepage:
   - A stunning hero section with high-quality culinary imagery, a clear value proposition, and a prominent "Contact Us" or "Enroll Now" Call to Action (CTA).
   - Summary of certified professional programs and fast-food workshops.
2. Courses & Trainings:
   - **Formation Pizzaïolo & Fast Food:** Interactive section detailing modules like Pizza 4 Saisons, Napolitaine, Margherita, Makloub, Baguette Farcie, Soufflé, Chappati, and Mlawi. Mention intensive practical training, professional supervision, and certificates.
   - **Formation en Cuisine et Pâtisserie:** Professional diploma programs (diplômé agréé par l'état) with complete modules on culinary arts, fine dining presentation, and French pastry.
3. About Us / Student Success:
   - Highlight graduation moments, practical kitchen training, and professional certifications that boost students' CVs and job readiness.
4. Contact & Location:
   - Address: 68 Rue ali belhouane, Kélibia, 8090, Tunisia
